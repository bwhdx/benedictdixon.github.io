Chapter I

Beyond the terminal

Engineering, 2015 to 2022: JPMorgan, American Express, MAIA, Icon

I started out as an engineer, building payment systems at JPMorgan, then on the R&D team for American Express’s referral campaigns. That’s where I first got to see beyond the terminal. We went into those design, user interview and research centres, behind the double glass, and got some exposure to the positioning and the marketing side as well. After that came a hedge fund, fintech, and founding my own startups.

Running companies, from 2021: Nettle, Pound Token, Voi Network, kash

Then I made a very conscious and intentional move from writing code to running companies and teams. When AI tooling arrived, I went back to building. Three of us built kash’s whole product. Mine was the fat middle, from the hooks the frontend calls down to indexing on-chain events.

I build end-to-end, full solutions, and I run and operate products and businesses completely end to end.
