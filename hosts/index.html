Bio

I started out as an engineer, building payment systems at JPMorgan, then on the R&D team for American Express’s referral campaigns. After that came a hedge fund, fintech, and founding my own startups. Then I made a very conscious and intentional move from writing code to running companies and teams.

Now Co-founder, COO and CTO at kash. A strategic, product and technology advisor to early-stage startups and founders.
